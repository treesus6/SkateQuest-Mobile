# Delete a SkateQuest Account

SkateQuest users can permanently delete their account from inside the app:

1. Sign in to SkateQuest.
2. Open **Profile**.
3. Tap **Delete Account Permanently**.
4. Confirm the two deletion prompts.

This removes the authentication account and account-linked SkateQuest data. Some community or security records may be retained only when reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or the integrity of shared records; identifying links to the deleted account are removed where appropriate.

## If you cannot access the app

You can request account and associated-data deletion without reinstalling SkateQuest. Use the **Developer contact** email shown on the SkateQuest Google Play listing and include:

- Subject: `SkateQuest account deletion request`
- The email address used for your SkateQuest account
- Your SkateQuest username, if known

Do **not** send your password. The developer may ask for a safe verification step before deleting an account to prevent unauthorized deletion requests.

For information about SkateQuest data practices, see [PRIVACY_POLICY.md](./PRIVACY_POLICY.md).
