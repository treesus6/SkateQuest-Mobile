Request Deletion of Selected Data

You can ask SkateQuest to delete selected personal data without deleting your entire SkateQuest account.

How to request deletion

Email support@skatequest.me from the email address associated with your SkateQuest account. Use the subject Delete My Data and identify the data you want removed.

Request Data Deletion

Data you can request to delete

  • Uploaded videos or clips
  • Photos you uploaded
  • Check-ins and location-linked activity
  • Trick logs and progression records
  • Session history and other account-linked activity
  • Other personal data linked to your account, where deletion is permitted

What stays

Your SkateQuest account, profile, and any data you do not ask us to delete will remain active. Some records may be retained when reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or the integrity of shared community records. Where appropriate, identifying links to your account are removed.

Want to delete the entire account instead?

Use our account deletion page for full account deletion instructions.
